# 🚗 Power BI Sales Dashboard – Automotive Industry

📌 Project Overview
This Power BI dashboard was created to analyze and visualize the sales performance of an automotive business. The dataset consists of multiple related tables, such as customers, employees, offices, orders, products, and payments. The goal of the dashboard is to provide key stakeholders with an interactive and insightful view of the business's sales, profits, product performance, and customer demographics.

❗ Problem & Solution
🔴 Business Challenges:
No unified view of sales and profitability across different product lines and regions.

Difficulty in identifying the top and bottom performing products.

Limited insights into which product lines contribute most to the overall revenue and profit.

Inability to analyze sales trends over time or by customer region.

Lack of clear KPIs for performance measurement.

✅ Dashboard Solution:
Developed a comprehensive Power BI dashboard to integrate data across all relevant business units.

Provided a dynamic view of total sales, profits, orders, and margin percentages.

Integrated filters for year, city, and employee to allow slice-and-dice capability.

Included visual breakdowns for best/worst products and customer location analysis.

🧩 Solutions Provided in the Dashboard
Sales Overview: Highlights key performance indicators like Total Sales, Profit, and Margin %.

Product Performance: Identifies best and worst selling products by sales value.

Yearly & Monthly Trends: Tracks sales quantity and profit changes across years and months.

Customer & Region Insights: Displays customer distribution on a global map to analyze geographic performance.

Category Analysis: Treemap and pie chart visuals show sales and quantity by product line.

⚙️ Tools & Technologies Used
Power BI Desktop – for dashboard creation and report publishing

Power Query – for data cleaning, merging, and transformation

DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – for creating calculated columns and measures

CSV/Excel Files – as data sources (products, orders, customers, payments, etc.)
